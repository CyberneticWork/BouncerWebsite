DEDICATED CRICKET ENVIRONMENT

Built for passionate cricketers, teams & learners

Bouncer Indoor Sports (Pvt.) Ltd. is an indoor cricket sports facility located at 125/A, Old Kesbewa Road, Delkanda, Nugegoda, Sri Lanka.

Whether you want to practise batting, improve your timing with bowling machine drills, or enjoy a softball cricket match with your team, our facilities provide space for individual practice, team training, and match play in an all-weather indoor environment.
